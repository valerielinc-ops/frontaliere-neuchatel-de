Vollständige Beschreibung

Want to help build a new warehouse and shape its safety culture from the start? We’re opening our site in Neuchâtel next year and are looking for someone who actively supports managers and employees in developing workplace safety and health protection.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Neuenburg am Rhein, Hans-Buck-Straße 3, 7939 Neuenburg am Rhein

Key information

We welcome all applications, but can only consider applications submitted online. Applications submitted by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Leading the safety team in Neuenburg (ON1 and ON2)
  • Point of contact for concerns and issues relating to health and safety at the site, supporting the responsible safety officer (if you have not yet completed your training)
  • Collaborating with internal and external stakeholders, authorities, auditors and service providers
  • Implementing projects in the field of occupational health and safety, and ensuring these aspects are taken into account in the implementation of general projects
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory and internal requirements in the field of occupational health and safety in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders and support functions (Sifa, fire safety officer, etc.)
  • Conducting, developing and refining training programmes, briefings, risk assessments, incident and accident analyses and audits, and providing support in the implementation and follow-up of the resulting measures
  • Raising staff awareness of safety and general compliance issues, and implementing, establishing and embedding a safety culture

Requisiti

  • 2-3 Years: Sifa- Fachkraft für Arbeitssicherheit
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): Sifa- Fachkraft für Arbeitssicherheit
  • Leadership experience and keen to build a team over the long term
  • Qualification as an occupational safety specialist (Sifa- Fachkraft für Arbeitssicherheit)
  • Strong communication skills and a hands-on approach
  • Experience in an operational or logistics environment, ideally in the field of health and safety
  • Very good written and spoken German and English
  • French would also be an advantage
  • Team spirit and a willingness to work with relevant stakeholders to find meaningful solutions
  • Identifying opportunities for improvement and constantly striving to further optimise health and safety
  • Analytical thinking and a structured, independent and solution-oriented approach to work come naturally to you
  • A strong sense of responsibility and integrity are hallmarks of your character
  • Build trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • Use creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • Training as a fire safety officer (Brandschutzbeauftragte/r)
  • German (Very good knowledge)
  • English (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Values, not empty words: Cooperative, innovative, piratesque, responsible, ambitious – these five values are what we practice and preach.
  • Courageous thinking: We encourage everyone to think on their feet, and love it when they come up with bold or edgy ideas.
  • Uncomplicated approach: Simplicity is at our core. We avoid the unnecessary and focus on what really matters – from overcoming challenges to celebrating our shared successes.
  • Flexibility and freedom (Office Jobs): We offer working-from-home options, up to 8 weeks of workation, and every position is possible with an 80% workload.
  • Rapid growth: With us, growth is a continuous journey. We also support you with EDU Points (CHF 2,000 for employees in Switzerland, EUR 1,600 for employees in Germany, and EUR 700 for our Serbian employees) for your professional development, so there’s no risk of you getting stuck in a rut.
  • More Benefits Germany: More Benefits Germany: 30 days of holiday, exclusive employee conditions in our online shop, a shuttle service (for Neuenburg am Rhein) and other benefits.

Brauche ich eine Grenzgänger-Bewilligung für eine Stelle im Kanton Neuenburg?

EU/EFTA-Bürger mit Wohnsitz in einem EU/EFTA-Staat können eine G-Bewilligung beantragen und in der ganzen Schweiz arbeiten: Grenzzonen gelten für sie nicht mehr, und sie müssen mindestens einmal pro Woche heimkehren. Der Schweizer Arbeitgeber reicht den Antrag nach Vertragsunterzeichnung beim Migrationsamt des Kantons Neuenburg ein. Die Bearbeitungszeiten variieren je nach Kanton — klären Sie die Details mit dem Migrationsamt des Kantons Neuenburg oder mit HR während der Bewerbung.
